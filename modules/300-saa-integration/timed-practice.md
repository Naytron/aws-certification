# Timed Practice

## Exam simulation

The official exam has 65 questions in 130 minutes: an average of two minutes per question. Fifteen questions are unscored and not identified, so treat every item as scored.

## Three-pass protocol

1. **Pass 1 - 75 minutes:** answer clear items; flag uncertain items. Do not spend more than about 90 seconds while stuck.
2. **Pass 2 - 40 minutes:** solve flagged items using written requirement elimination.
3. **Pass 3 - 15 minutes:** fill every blank and verify multiple-response counts. There is no penalty for guessing; unanswered items are incorrect.

For shorter original sets, preserve two minutes per item and reserve the final 15% for review.

## Practice ladder

| Stage | Format | Gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | 10-15 untimed original scenarios | Explain every rejection |
| B | 20-30 mixed questions at 2.25 min/item | At least 80%, no domain below 70% |
| C | 65-question fresh simulation in 130 minutes | Meet scorecard gates |
| D | Second fresh simulation after at least 48 hours | Meet gates again without answer recall |

## During the set

- Record confidence: H, M, or L.
- Do not consult notes.
- Do not pause the clock except for a real testing accommodation.
- Guess before time expires.
- Never change an answer solely from anxiety; change it only after identifying a missed requirement or false assumption.

## After the set

Review all incorrect items and every correct low-confidence item. Track accuracy by official domain, time-pressure errors, and lucky guesses. A repeated set is remediation, not readiness evidence.
