## Risks

## Defense questions

1. Which hard requirement rules out the strongest alternative?
2. Where is the largest blast radius?
3. What happens when an AZ, Region, identity provider, or network link fails?
4. How is unauthorized access prevented and detected?
5. How is the workload restored, and when was that tested?
6. Which cost grows fastest with success?
7. What is the rollback trigger?
8. Which assumption would invalidate the design?
