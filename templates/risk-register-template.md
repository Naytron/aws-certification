## Scoring convention

Define likelihood and impact consistently before ranking. A numeric score without a shared definition is not evidence.

## Review prompts

- Which risk crosses an account, AZ, Region, or organizational boundary?
- Which risk can silently consume credits or budget?
- Which mitigation creates a new operational dependency?
- Which residual risk needs business acceptance?
